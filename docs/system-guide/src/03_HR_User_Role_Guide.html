1. Role at a glance

HR User is the individual recruiter. You have a personal workspace: every job you create, every candidate the platform finds for you and every score, note and status you set belongs to you (your HR Manager and Admins can look, but not change).

Scope in one sentence

You see and change only your own jobs and candidates. Nothing you do affects a colleague’s data, and nothing a colleague does affects yours.

CAN SEE

  • Your own dashboard, jobs and candidates
  • Match scores and AI summaries for your candidates
  • Your own notes on each candidate
  • Reports scoped to “My work”
  • Your account e-mail (Settings)
  • Who last opened each of your sourcing files

CAN DO

  • Create jobs from an uploaded or pasted job description
  • Run AI analysis and edit the extracted requirements
  • Start candidate searches (Egypt)
  • Score candidates with AI
  • Filter, sort, page and export candidates to Excel
  • Change candidate status, add notes
  • Delete jobs and candidates you own
  • Export your own reports as CSV or Excel

CANNOT DO

  • See other people’s jobs, candidates or dashboards
  • Open the Team (/manager) or Admin (/admin) areas
  • See the notification bell (managers and admins only)
  • Invite users or change roles
  • Edit a job after creation (no edit screen)
  • Edit or delete a note once saved
  • Change your password or profile in Settings

2. Getting access and signing in

2.1 How you get access

The platform is not open to everyone. Access exists only when your account is marked active. There are two routes to it:

Route A: you are invited (recommended)

  1. An Admin enters your e-mail on the Admin page and picks the role HR User and your team.
  2. You open the login page and sign up (or use Google) with that exact e-mail.
  3. Your account is created already active, with the role and team the Admin chose. You land on the Dashboard.

Route B: you ask for access

  1. You sign up with an e-mail nobody invited.
  2. The account is created inactive and every Admin is notified of an access request.
  3. Until an Admin approves it, signing in shows: “Your account doesn’t have access yet. Ask an admin to invite you or approve your access request.”
There is no “rejected” message

Admins have an Approve button but no Reject button. If nobody approves your request you simply keep seeing the “doesn’t have access yet” message. Contact an Admin directly.

Access can be removed while you are working

If an Admin deactivates your account, your next page load signs you out and shows the “doesn’t have access” message. Any action already in progress in the browser is refused with “Unauthorized”.

4.1 Exactly how every number is computed

CardWhat it countsWatch out for
Active JobsNumber of jobs you own. It is not filtered by any “active” flag: every job you ever created, minus ones you deleted.The name says “Active” but it is your total job count.
Total CandidatesNumber of candidate records you own. Candidates are stored once per person for you, even if found for several jobs.A person found for two jobs counts once.
Average Match QualityThe match scores are grouped by search run; each run’s scores are averaged; then those per-run averages are averaged and rounded. Every scored sourcing file therefore weighs the same, big or small. If nothing is scored, the card says “No evaluated files yet”.Scores with no search run attached are ignored here. Runs with no scores are left out. Stays empty until you press Score Candidates.
Shortlisted TalentCandidates you own whose status is currently Shortlisted.Moving a candidate to Hired removes them from this count.

Candidate Pipeline Stages

A badge shows “N total”. Horizontal bars show how many of your candidates currently have each status, in this order: New, Reviewed, Shortlisted, Contacted, Hired, Rejected. Bars are sized relative to the biggest stage (with a small minimum width); a stage with zero has no bar. Status is a property of the candidate itself, so the chart is across all your jobs. Empty state: “No candidates yet — run a search to start building your pipeline.”

Match Score Distribution

A five-bar histogram of every match score you own, in buckets 0-20, 21-40, 41-60, 61-80, 81-100. A green badge shows the overall average (“68% avg”). Hover a bar to see “61-80%: N candidate(s)”. Empty state: “No candidates scored yet — run ‘Score Candidates’ on a job to see the match-quality breakdown here.”

Two averages can differ

The Average Match Quality card averages per sourcing file, and skips scores that have no run attached. The distribution chart badge includes all scores. Small differences between the two are expected.

Today’s Searched Candidates

Badge “N today” (green when above zero) counts candidate records created since midnight (server time, which is UTC when hosted on Vercel, not your local midnight). The list shows the latest 8: name, headline, the job the candidate was first linked to, and the time. Empty: “No candidates sourced today yet”.

Two things to know

(1) A person who already existed and is found again today is not counted, only brand-new records. (2) Clicking a name opens the profile without a job, so there is no Match Score card and the candidate is not marked as seen. To see the score, open the candidate from the job’s candidate list instead.

6.2 Stage B: Job Description

Upload File

Accepted files

  • PDF, DOCX, TXT
  • Maximum size 10 MB
  • Type is decided by the file name ending (.pdf, .docx, .txt)
  • Old Word .doc files are not supported: save as DOCX or PDF

What happens after you choose a file

  1. Toast “Extracting text from <file>...” and the card reads “Parsing text from <file>...”.
  2. Text is extracted on the server. Toast “Upload Successful”.
  3. The card shows the file name, size and “Ready for analysis”, plus an “Extracted Text Preview” box.

After a successful upload you can press Change File to pick another or the X icon to remove the file (this also clears the extracted text). You can drag and drop, but a dropped file of the wrong type is only rejected by the server after it is sent.

Scanned PDFs extract as empty

The platform does not read images. A scanned or image-only PDF gives an empty result, the description stays empty and Analyze Job with AI stays disabled without any message. Open the PDF, copy the text out (or re-export it from Word) and use Paste Text.

Paste Text

Click Paste Text to get a large text box with the placeholder “Paste the full job description here (responsibilities, technical requirements, qualifications)...”. Under it, “Minimum 50 characters required” and a live character counter. There is no maximum.

Better description, better search

Include responsibilities, must-have skills, years of experience, education and seniority. The AI only uses what is written; it does not guess missing requirements.

Analyze Job with AI

  1. The button is disabled while analyzing or while the description is shorter than 50 characters (after trimming).
  2. Press it. Both cards fold, a loader card reads “Analyzing Job Description...” and a toast tells you the AI is working.
  3. The AI reads the description and returns a structured profile (chapter 10). Toast: “Requirements successfully extracted from job spec!”
  4. The Extracted Requirements card (stage C) appears with badge “Analysis Verified”.
If the analysis fails

The cards stay folded and only a toast appears. Reopen the Job Description card to see the red message under it. Common ones: “Job description is too short to analyze.”, “The AI service is currently unavailable. Please try again shortly.”, “The AI returned an unexpected response. Please try again.”, or “Too many requests. Please slow down and try again shortly.” (limit: 10 AI requests per minute, shared with query generation and scoring).

6.4 Stage D: Generate Queries & Find Candidates

The green Generate Queries & Find Candidates button lives under “Ready to Find Candidates?”. It is disabled while a search is running or when Job Details is incomplete; in that case the message says “Complete all required job details (title, company, employment type, work arrangement, location) above to continue.” If you still trigger it, you get “Please complete all required job details before continuing.”, a “Missing Required Fields” toast, and Job Details reopens.

What happens, step by step

  1. Query generation: the AI writes 2 to 4 Google “X-ray” queries aimed at LinkedIn profiles, from your title, seniority, skills and location. Status text: “Synthesizing multi-variable search queries...”. You cannot see or edit the queries.
  2. Save the job: your job (title, company, Egypt, city, type, arrangement, seniority, skills, experience, education, certifications, languages, keywords and the full AI analysis) is saved to your account. Status text: “Querying authorized talent pools across public sources...”
  3. Start the search run: a run is created with status pending and the platform answers immediately. The heavy work continues on the server in the background.
  4. Polling: the page asks for the run’s status every 2 seconds, for up to 10 minutes. The button shows a spinner and “Finding Candidates...”. (The label “Deduplicating candidates and computing AI match scores...” is misleading: no scoring happens here.)
  5. Completion: the success modal opens and a toast says “Found N candidates! Sourcing complete.”

What can go wrong

You seeMeaning and action

Retrying creates a duplicate job

The job is saved before the search starts. If the search step fails (for example a rate limit) and you press the button again, a second job is created. Check the Jobs list or the candidates job picker afterwards and delete the extra one (chapter 7). To re-search a job you already saved, see chapter 13.

Leave the page?

Once the run has started, the search continues on the server even if you close the tab. Return to the Dashboard and use View on the Sourcing Files row when its status turns complete.

It re-scores everyone, every time

Manager alert on strong matches

If at least one newly scored candidate reaches 80% or more, your HR Manager is notified that you scored candidates with strong matches.

Pick a value in the row’s Status dropdown. It saves at once (no confirmation) and a toast confirms “Candidate status marked as ‘<status>’”. Status belongs to the candidate, so it applies in every job that person is linked to and changes the Dashboard counts. Meanings are in chapter 9.

Viewing and deleting a candidate

The eye icon opens the profile. The trash icon shows an inline Delete? with Confirm and Cancel.

Deleting a candidate is permanent and global

The toast says “Candidate removed from this job.” but the whole candidate record is deleted from your account, including their notes, scores, status history and links to all your jobs. It is not just removed from this list. There is no undo.

Pagination and Export

The footer shows “Page X of Y (N total)” with Previous and Next. Export Excel is described next.

Every candidate has exactly one status. It starts as New and you change it by hand. The statuses are, in dropdown order:

Order on the Dashboard chart

New, Reviewed, Shortlisted, Contacted, Hired, Rejected. (The dropdown order differs: Rejected sits before Contacted.)

Who is notified

Only changes to Shortlisted or Hired notify your team’s HR Manager (if you belong to a team with an active manager). You never receive notifications yourself. Setting the same status again does nothing and is not logged.

Suggested flow

New → Reviewed → Shortlisted → Contacted → Hired, with Rejected as the exit at any point. Keeping statuses current makes the Pipeline chart and your reports meaningful.

10. How the AI pipeline works

You do not need to understand the internals to use the platform, but knowing what each stage does explains why results look the way they do.

Stage by stage

StageWhat really happens
1. Text extractionPDF, DOCX or TXT is converted to plain text on the server. No image reading (OCR).
2. AI analysisAn AI model (OpenAI) reads the description and returns a profile: job title, seniority, employment type, required and preferred skills, minimum/maximum years of experience, education, certifications, languages, industries, keywords and responsibilities. It is told to use only what is in the text. Location is then forced to Egypt.
3. Query generationThe AI writes 2 to 4 Google search strings that target LinkedIn profiles. Each contains a group of title synonyms (3 to 6), one or two short quoted skill phrases and a quoted location. If the job already has earlier queries, new ones avoid repeating them.
4. SearchEvery query is run on Google through a search service (SerpApi), all at once. Requests are biased to Egypt (Google Egypt, country code EG). If you chose a City, the location parameter is that city. Each query normally returns about 10 organic results, so 2 to 4 queries give roughly up to 40 raw results. Company pages, job boards (Indeed, Glassdoor, Bayt, Wuzzuf and similar), recruitment agencies and job postings are filtered out; individual profiles are kept.
5. EnrichmentWhen configured, each LinkedIn profile is read by an Apify scraper to obtain the real name, headline, current company, location, about text, skills and total years of experience. Enrichment overrides the thinner data from the search snippet. If it fails, the run still continues with search data only.
6. Location verificationFor Egypt-scoped jobs (all jobs), each candidate’s location is checked: text that mentions Egypt or one of Egypt’s 27 governorates passes; text naming somewhere else (for example Dubai) is dropped. With no location text, the AI judges from the headline and summary; “unknown” is kept. Verified people get the green Egypt verified badge.
7. Clean, dedupe, saveImplausible values are removed (for example a company equal to the group’s own name, or impossible years of experience). The same person is recognised by LinkedIn address (or other address, or name + company) and merged. If you already have that person, the existing record is only topped up (never overwritten) and reused; otherwise a new candidate with status New is created. All are linked to your job.
8. Scoring (manual)Only when you press Score Candidates. The AI compares each candidate’s profile with the job’s requirements and returns an overall match score 0-100 and five sub-scores (skills, experience, location, education, seniority), plus lists of matched and missing requirements, strengths, concerns and a summary.
Scoring is model-determined

There is no fixed formula or weighting in the platform. The overall score is whatever the AI returns as its weighted judgment, and the five sub-scores are the AI’s own. The same candidate may score slightly differently if re-scored. Treat scores as a ranking aid, not a verdict, and read the AI summary, missing requirements and concerns before deciding.

How the AI scores skills

Skills are matched by meaning, not exact words: JS = JavaScript, Postgres = PostgreSQL. An umbrella term such as “Front-End Development” can be satisfied by HTML, CSS and JS, with partial coverage split between matched and missing. It is told never to invent skills that are not on the profile.

Score colours

70 to 100 green 40 to 69 amber 0 to 39 red
“Strong match” for manager notification is 80 or above.

What the search cannot know

Google shows only public pages. It returns people whose public profile matches the wording of the queries; it is not a database of everyone in Egypt. Profiles with the location written as an abbreviation or a city not in the governorate list (for example “Alex”) may be dropped by the Egypt check.

13. Step-by-step tasks

Task 1: Run your first search

  1. Click New Job Opening on the Dashboard (or New Job on the Jobs page).
  2. Fill Job Title, Company, Employment Type and Work Arrangement.
  3. In Job Description, upload a PDF/DOCX/TXT (or choose Paste Text and paste at least 50 characters).
  4. Press Analyze Job with AI and wait for “Requirements successfully extracted from job spec!”.
  5. Review the Extracted Requirements: fix title and seniority, keep Egypt, optionally choose a City, set the experience range, prune and add skill chips on the three tabs.
  6. Press Generate Queries & Find Candidates. Wait while the button shows “Finding Candidates...” (usually up to a few minutes).
  7. When “Candidate Sourcing Complete!” appears, press Show Candidates.
  8. Press Score Candidates and wait for the success toast.
Before you press the big green button

Check that the Company and Employment Type are the ones you want: the job cannot be edited later.

Task 2: Search again for a job

There is no “run again” button and no edit screen for saved jobs. Searches can only be started from the New Job page, which always saves a new job. To search again (for example with a different City or refined skills):

  1. Open New Job and re-enter the details, then re-upload or re-paste the same description.
  2. Analyze, adjust the requirements as needed, and start the search.
  3. People you already hold are recognised and reused (their records are only topped up, not duplicated, and keep their status and notes); they are linked to the new job. New people are added.
  4. Score the new job’s candidates and, when you are happy, delete the old job from the job picker to keep your list tidy.
Old scores stay with the old job

Scores are stored per job and candidate. A new job starts unscored, even for people who were scored in the old job.

Task 3: Score and shortlist

  1. Open Candidates, click View on your job.
  2. Press Score Candidates and wait for “Match Scoring Completed”.
  3. Leave the sort at Match Score, direction High → Low.
  4. Open the top candidates with the eye icon or name link. Read the AI summary, missing requirements and concerns; open View profile to check the real profile.
  5. Set the status to Shortlisted (or Rejected) directly in the Status dropdown.
  6. To view only your shortlist, set the Status filter to Shortlisted.

Task 4: Add notes

  1. Open the candidate from the job’s list.
  2. Scroll to Recruiter Notes, type the note.
  3. Press Add Note and wait for the “saved” toast. The note shows at the top with date and time.

Task 5: Export

  1. Open the job’s candidate list.
  2. Set filters and sort the way you want the spreadsheet (type text filters, then press Apply Filters).
  3. Press Export Excel. The file candidates-<title>-<date>.xlsx downloads with all matching rows across pages.
  4. To export a report instead, use CSV or Excel on /reports.
Score first, export second

Score columns are blank for unscored candidates, so score before exporting a list you plan to share.

Task 6: Clean up jobs

  1. Open Candidates to see the job picker (20 newest jobs).
  2. Click the trash icon next to the job you no longer need, then Confirm.
  3. Toast “Job and its listing removed.” confirms it. Candidates remain in your account.
  4. To remove individual candidates, open the job’s list and use the row trash icon (permanent, all jobs).
Check before you delete

Export the list first if you may need the scores or notes later; deletion of a job or a candidate cannot be undone.

15. Troubleshooting FAQ and gotchas

Frequently asked questions

Question / symptomAnswer
I searched but every score is empty and the Dashboard average says “No evaluated files yet”.The search never scores. Open the job’s candidate list and press Score Candidates. The modal wording “identified and evaluated” is misleading.
The search failed and I pressed the button again: now I have two jobs.The job is saved before the search starts, so each retry creates a new job. Delete the duplicate from the Candidates job picker.
The toast says the candidate was removed “from this job”. Is it only removed here?No. The candidate is deleted from your whole account, with notes, scores and links to all jobs. It cannot be undone.
I cannot find an older job on the Candidates page.The picker lists only the 20 newest jobs. Use Jobs and its View Candidates link (10 per page).
I opened a candidate from “Today’s Searched Candidates” and there is no Match Score.That link skips the job. Open the candidate from the job’s list to see the score; that also marks the candidate as seen.
The Unseen number never goes down.A candidate becomes seen only when opened with the job attached (from the candidate list).
Export Excel replaced my page with unreadable text.The export failed (usually too many exports in a minute, or a temporary error) and the link shows the error text. Press Back and retry.
My Excel is missing rows I typed a filter for.Only applied filters are exported. Press Apply Filters first.
Analyze Job with AI is greyed out after uploading a PDF.Probably a scanned PDF (image only): the extracted text is empty. Copy the text out and use Paste Text. Also check the description is 50+ characters.

A candidate I know is in Egypt is missing.The Egypt check drops locations that do not mention Egypt or one of its governorates (for example a bare city or abbreviation).
A run shows “running” for a very long time.The background job may have been stopped by the platform on long runs. After roughly 10 minutes, start the search again on a new job and tell your Admin.

Today’s count is 0 but I searched today.Only brand-new candidate records count, and “today” follows server time (UTC).

I cannot see a Team or Admin menu.Those are for HR Managers and Admins. Your role is HR User.
