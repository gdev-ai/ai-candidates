Based on the application code and the live database policies. Terms: a sourcing file is one sourcing run on a job; its owner is the owner of that job. Names shown in the app are derived from e-mail addresses (zeyad.ragab@... becomes "Zeyad Ragab").

1  Role at a glance

The HR Manager role is the middle level of the platform. A manager is a normal recruiter who additionally has management-level visibility over one team: the team they are assigned to by an Admin. Team members keep ownership of their own jobs, candidates and files; the manager can look at everything but change none of it.

Scope in one sentence

Everything you see about other people is limited to your own team (the profiles that share your team assignment, including yourself and any inactive members). Other teams do not exist for you: their pages return "not found" and their data is blocked by the database.

Can see

  • Team Dashboard: totals, pipeline, runs, member cards, comparison, activity
  • Every sourcing file owned by your team
  • Each teammate's page: stats, pipeline, files, recent activity
  • Teammates' jobs, candidates, match scores and recruiter notes (read-only)
  • Sign-in and last-activity times of teammates
  • Team reports (4 types) with CSV / Excel export
  • Notification bell for team milestones

Can do

  • Everything an HR User does with your own jobs and candidates: create jobs, run sourcing, score, change status, add notes, export
  • Open a teammate's file or candidate in read-only mode
  • Filter the team files table; filter and page a teammate's candidate list
  • Export a teammate's candidate list to Excel
  • Filter, view and export team reports
  • Mark notifications read

Cannot do

  • Edit, delete, re-score or change the status of a teammate's jobs or candidates; add notes to them
  • Run a search on a teammate's job
  • Change roles, teams, activation; invite people; use /admin
  • See another team, or the whole organization
  • Use the team picker or organization-wide report scope
  • Delete or edit the activity log

The mental model

A team is assigned by an Admin

If no team is assigned to your profile, the Team Dashboard shows "You're not assigned to a team yet" and reports fall back to "My work" (only yourself). Ask an Admin to assign you.

3  Your own workspace

A manager is also a recruiter. All personal pages behave exactly like the HR User's; see the HR User Role Guide for the full detail. They never show team data, even for a manager: every query is filtered to your own user.

PageWhat it shows for a manager
/dashboardPersonal "Candidate Sourcing Hub": four stat cards, pipeline stages, match score distribution, today's searched candidates and a "Sourcing Files" table of your 20 latest runs (no owner column, no "Not owner" tag).
/jobsYour own jobs only (10 per page) with "New Job" and "View Candidates". Team jobs are not listed here.
/jobs/newCreate a job: upload or paste a job description, AI analysis, edit requirements, then "Generate Queries & Find Candidates".
/candidatesCandidate list per job with filters, "Score Candidates", status changes and "Export Excel". Without a job in the address you get a job picker listing only your own jobs.
/reportsFor a manager this is the team report scope (section 7).
/settingsAccount card only.
Two different views of "your" numbers

On files you own you keep every power of an HR User; the read-only rules in section 6 apply only to other people's files. The personal dashboard counts only what you own. The Team Dashboard counts what everyone in the team owns, including you. The numbers on the two screens will therefore differ, and both are correct.

4.1 Hero banner and error states

  • Pill "Team view", the team name as title, and the text "Managed by name · N active member(s)". The managing person is the member recorded as the team's manager; otherwise "No manager assigned".
  • A red banner "Some team data failed to load. Figures below may be incomplete." appears if any underlying query failed. Reload; if it persists, tell an Admin.
  • Full-page states: "You're not assigned to a team yet" (no team on your profile) and "Team unavailable" ("This team doesn't exist or you don't have access to it.").

4.2 The four stat cards and how they are computed

CardHow it is computed
Team Members"active / total", for example 3 / 4. Total = every profile assigned to your team (you, active and inactive members). Active = those switched on.
Team JobsSum of the jobs owned by all team members, all-time (no date window).
Average Match QualityEach sourcing file gets an average of its candidates' match scores; the card averages those file averages, so every file counts equally regardless of size. Files with no scored candidate are left out. Shows "No evaluated files yet" when there are none.
Shortlisted TalentCandidates whose status is currently "Shortlisted". Contacted and Hired candidates are not counted here.

Exact meaning of "Unseen by you"

It counts the candidates in your team's files for which you personally have not yet opened the candidate detail page from that job (the address contains ?jobId=). It is not what the file owner has or has not seen; the owner's own unseen numbers appear on the owner's dashboard. Only opening a candidate's detail page (via the eye icon or the name link in a job's candidate list) marks it seen for you. Merely opening the file list does not.

Because this counts the newest team files only (see section 13), it is a "how much of the team's output have I personally reviewed" indicator, not a workload measure.

4.5 Team Members cards

Description: "Open a member to see their performance, files, and activity". The whole card is a link to the member page (section 5). The team manager's card comes first, the rest by name.

  • Header: initials avatar, name, subtitle "Team Manager" or the role label, plus " · Inactive" for switched-off accounts.
  • Three mini-stats: Active files (runs pending or running), Candidates, Shortlisted.
  • Footer: "N file(s) · last active relative time" (the latest of jobs, runs, candidate updates, logged activity and file opens, all-time; "—" if none) and a match badge "N% match": green from 70%, amber 40-69%, red below 40%, hidden when there is no score.
  • Empty: "No team members yet".

4.6 Team Sourcing Files: filters

Description: "Every sourcing file owned by your team. Opening one is recorded as an access by you, not a change of owner." The badge shows how many rows are displayed. The form uses plain web-address parameters, so a filtered view can be bookmarked.

FilterBehaviour
Team member"Everyone" or one person (includes yourself).
Job titleText, "Any title"; matches part of the title, ignoring case; max 120 characters.
StatusAny status, Pending, Running, Complete, Failed.
CreatedAll time (default on this screen), Today, Last 7 days, Last 30 days, Last 90 days, Custom range. Windows count whole days including today; a custom range includes both end dates.
From / ToDates. Entering dates without choosing Custom is treated as a custom range.
Min match %0-100. Files whose average match is at least this value. Files without any score are excluded when this is set.
Min candidatesFiles with at least this many linked candidates.
Apply / ResetApply reloads with the filters. Reset appears only when a filter is active and returns to /manager.

Invalid values (unknown status, bad numbers) are silently ignored rather than reported.

4.8 Member Comparison

Description: "Output per team member — runs shown as completed / total". Columns: User (link to the member page, with role and "Inactive"), Jobs, Runs ("completed / total"), Candidates, Shortlisted, Contacted, Rejected, Avg Match, Last Login, Last Activity. All values are all-time.

Description: "Latest recorded actions by your team". The 20 newest logged actions of all team members including yours, newest first, written as "Actor what they did" with a relative time. Typical entries: job created, run started/completed/failed, candidate status changed, note added, candidates scored, sign-ins, report viewed/exported and "Opened Owner's sourcing file". How the access is recorded

Access rowWritten on every open that carries a run id. A file you are not allowed to see yields "Sourcing run not found." and nothing is recorded.
"Not owner" badgeShown next to "Last Accessed By" when the latest opener is not the file's owner.
Activity entryWritten only when you are not the owner and you have not opened the same file in the past 30 minutes (de-duplication). Text: Opened <Owner>'s sourcing file "<title>".
NotificationsOpening a file notifies nobody.
OwnershipNever changes. Data never changes.
Access is not recorded when there is no run id

The "View Candidates" link in /jobs and job-picker links have no run id, and a "job scored" notification opens the job without a run id. Those views are read-only if the job is a teammate's, but leave no access trace. Candidate-level notifications open the candidate without a job, so no Match Score card is shown.

Actions by type: Action, Events, Members (most frequent first).

Recruitment Pipeline

Where sourced candidates currently sit in the pipeline.

Tiles: Total candidates, New, Reviewed, Shortlisted, Contacted, Rejected, Hired.
Pipeline by member: Member, Total, New, Reviewed, Shortlisted, Contacted, Rejected, Hired, Progressed (%; share now shortlisted, contacted or hired); totals row.

Sourcing Quality

Average AI match quality by member, job, and sourcing file.

Tiles: Average match, Files evaluated ("evaluated / files"), Strong files (70% and above), Weak files (below 40%).
By member: Member, Evaluated files, Avg match; "Team average" row.
By job: Job, Owner, Files, Evaluated, Avg match, Best file.
By sourcing file: Job, Owner, Status, Created, Candidates, Shortlisted, Avg match.

Export and logging

  • CSV and Excel export the currently selected report, period and (for you) your team. File name: <title>-report-<scope>-<date>.csv|xlsx. CSV opens cleanly in Excel (UTF-8 marker) and begins with title, scope, period and generated-at lines, then each section with its header and total rows; percentage columns are labelled "(%)". Excel has an "Overview" sheet plus one sheet per section.
  • Cells that start with =, +, - or @ are prefixed with an apostrophe to prevent spreadsheet formula injection.
  • Exports are limited to 20 per minute. If a report cannot be built you see "This report couldn't be generated. Try again in a moment."
  • Logging: viewing a report logs "Viewed the … report (scope, range)" (repeat views with the same description within 10 minutes are logged once). Each CSV/Excel download logs "Exported the … report … as CSV". Both appear in the Reports column of the Activity report and in the Team Activity feed.
Activity history starts September 2026

The activity log began recording in September 2026, so older periods show few or no logged actions in the Activity report. Sourcing runs are counted from the runs themselves and are complete.

8  Notifications

The bell (visible to HR Managers and Admins) tells you when something meaningful happens in your team so you do not have to keep checking the dashboard. There are no e-mail or push notifications: alerts exist only inside the application.

Bell behaviour

  • The red badge shows the unread count (capped at 99+). The accessible name is "Notifications, N unread".
  • It refreshes every 60 seconds while the browser tab is visible, again when you return to the tab and each time you open it.
  • The dropdown "Notifications" lists the 20 latest items. Each shows an unread dot, "Actor what happened" and a relative time; unread rows are tinted.
  • Click an item: it is marked read and you go to its destination. Mark all as read appears only when something is unread. Esc or a click outside closes the panel.
  • Empty: "You're all caught up. Team sourcing runs, shortlists, hires, strong matches, and access requests will show up here." On error: "Notifications couldn't be loaded. Try again shortly."

Events that notify you

When a teammate (not you) in your team does one of these, every active manager of that team is notified:

EventOpens
A sourcing run startsThe file (read-only), access recorded
A sourcing run completesThe file (read-only), access recorded
A sourcing run failsThe file (read-only), access recorded
A candidate is set to Shortlisted or HiredThe candidate page (no match card)
Scoring finds strong matches (80% or higher, at least one)The job's candidate list (no run id)

Does NOT notify you

  • Your own actions, ever
  • Someone opening a file (including yours)
  • Jobs created, updated or deleted
  • Notes added
  • Status changes to Reviewed, Contacted or Rejected
  • Sign-ins or failed sign-ins
  • Report views and exports
  • Team or role changes by an Admin
  • Access requests (these go to Admins only)
Fallback destination

If a notification has no specific target it opens the acting member's page (/manager/team/<member>). Notification items are private to you; you can only mark them read, never delete them. An item disappears if the underlying activity is no longer readable by you.

10  Step-by-step tasks

10.1 Review team performance weekly

  1. Click Team. Reload the page first if it was already open, since it never refreshes itself.
  2. Check the red banner (data errors), then the stat cards for the overall picture.
  3. In Sourcing Runs, look at Failed and Running. Filter the files table by Status "Failed" to find the affected files.
  4. In Member Comparison, compare Runs, Candidates, Shortlisted and Avg Match, and check Last Login / Last Activity for quiet members.
  5. Open Reports → Team Performance (Last 7 days) for the week's numbers, and export it to CSV if you need to share them.

10.2 Investigate a member

  1. On the Team Dashboard click the member's card (or their name in Member Comparison).
  2. Read the hero (status, last login, last activity) and the four stat cards.
  3. Study the pipeline and Performance Overview: many Failed runs or low match average point to a search-quality problem.
  4. Scan Sourcing Files and Recent Activity. Click View on a file to inspect the candidates read-only.
  5. To act on what you find, talk to the member or ask an Admin; you cannot change their data.

10.3 See who opened or created a file

  1. Find the file in Team Sourcing Files (use the Team member and Job title filters).
  2. Created by: read the line under the job title ("Created Sep 3 by ..."). It names the starter when they differ from the owner.
  3. Owner: the "Owned By" column.
  4. Last opened by: the "Last Accessed By" column; an indigo "Not owner" tag means someone other than the owner.
  5. For a full history of opens, use the Team Activity feed ("Opened ...'s sourcing file") or the Activity report column "Teammate files opened". Repeat opens within 30 minutes by the same person appear once.

10.4 Find high-quality candidates

  1. In the files filter set Min match % (for example 70) and optionally Min candidates and a date range, then Apply.
  2. Sort your attention by the green Match badges and the Shortlisted count.
  3. Click View. In the candidate list use the Status filter and sort to bring the best matches to the top.
  4. Open a candidate (eye icon) to read the Match Score breakdown, strengths, concerns and recruiter notes.
  5. Use Export Excel to take the filtered list with you. Recommend actions to the owner, who alone can change the status.
Also handy

The Sourcing Quality report ranks jobs and files by average match and lists the best file per job.

10.5 Export a team report

  1. Open Reports and pick the report tab.
  2. Choose the Period (default Last 30 days) or a custom From / To, then Update report.
  3. Check the scope line ("Everyone in your team") and the summary tiles.
  4. Click CSV or Excel. The file downloads and the export is logged.

10.6 Follow a notification

  1. Click the bell. Unread items are tinted with a dot.
  2. Click the item, for example "Karim Mostafa completed a sourcing run".
  3. You land on the file in read-only mode (run notifications), the candidate page (shortlist/hire) or the job list (strong matches).
  4. The item is now read. Use Mark all as read to clear the rest.

11  What you cannot do, and how it is enforced

Restrictions are enforced in three independent layers, so hiding a button is never the only protection.

You cannot …Enforcement
Edit, delete or re-run a teammate's jobUI: not offered. API: 403 "You can view this job, but only its owner can change it." on job update/delete and on starting a search. Database: writes owner-only.
Score candidates, change status, delete candidates or add notes on a teammate's fileUI: buttons hidden / status as text. API: same 403 text for "candidate". Database: owner-only write policies.
Change roles, teams, activation; invite users; create or rename teamsUI: no /admin link, /admin redirects non-admins. API: admin-only endpoints. Database: profile and team updates admin-only.
See another teamDatabase: managers see only profiles and data of their own team (via active-manager rules). Pages: URL team id ignored; a member outside your team gives "not found".
Use org-wide reports or the team pickerServer chooses scope from your role; the picker and "Report scope" only render for Admins.
See what others have openedThe "seen" tracking is private per viewer, so "Unseen" is always relative to you.
Change or delete the activity logIt is append-only; you can only write entries about yourself.
Delete notificationsYou may only set the read time of your own notifications.
Deactivation removes manager powers instantly

If an Admin deactivates a manager, the elevated database access stops and the next request signs them out.

13  Good-to-know limitations

Team files table

  • No column sorting, no pagination and no export; newest first, at most 200 files.
  • The member page shows at most 50 files with no filters.
  • Filter values that are invalid are silently ignored.
  • The "Created" filter defaults to All time here, unlike reports (30 days).

Numbers

  • Dashboard KPIs (runs, Unseen by you, Contacted/Rejected candidates) cover the newest 200 files (500 when filters are active), not the entire history.
  • Stat cards, Member Comparison and member pages are all-time; reports use a period.
  • "Unseen" is per viewer, never per owner.
  • Shortlisted counts only the current status; Hired candidates are not included.
  • Match figures average per file, so a tiny file weighs as much as a large one.

Finding teammates' work

  • The job picker on /candidates and the /jobs list show only your own jobs. Reach team files via /manager, member pages or notifications.
  • Opening via "View Candidates" (job list) or a job-scored notification records no access.
  • The candidate list is per job, not per run: it shows all candidates of the job even when you arrive from one run.
  • Candidate notifications open without the match card.

Other

  • Names are derived from e-mail addresses; there is no separate name field.
  • Pages do not auto-refresh; notifications poll every 60 seconds.
  • No e-mail or push notifications.
  • Managers cannot reassign files, change ownership, or edit members.
  • Exporting a teammate's candidates to Excel is allowed but is not logged.
  • Access-log entries collapse repeat opens by the same person within 30 minutes.
  • Activity history starts September 2026.
Rule of thumb

Use the Team Dashboard to see, the member page to understand, the read-only file view to inspect, reports to share, and the bell to be told. To change anything belonging to someone else, ask the owner or an Admin.
